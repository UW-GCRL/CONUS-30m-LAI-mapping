What it is

A multi-decadal 30 m LAI record for the contiguous United States

CONUS-30m-LAI provides monthly Leaf Area Index at 30-meter resolution for vegetated land in the contiguous United States, every month from January 2000 through December 2022. At 30 m, individual crop fields and forest stands that are mixed within 250 m to 1 km pixels can be separated.

LAI is retrieved from Landsat 5, 7, 8, and 9 Collection 2 surface reflectance using the biome-stratified Random Forest algorithm of Kang et al. (2021), which uses MODIS LAI as training reference data to retrieve LAI from Landsat observations. Monthly composites are built by pixel-wise median and mosaicked to a common 30 m grid.

Because clouds and sparse acquisitions leave gaps, mostly in winter, we also release a gap-filled companion product. A climatology-anchored temporal method fills gaps in pixel-years with at least one retrieval, keeps every retrieved value unchanged, and a per-pixel QA layer labels each filled value.

How good is it

Accuracy

Checks of the delivered product: against field measurements at 27 NEON sites, against 20 m reference maps, and for consistency with MODIS LAI, which also supplied the training targets.

0.78
R² vs field PAI, 6,195 plot-dates at 27 NEON sites (this product)
1.06
RMSE vs field (m² m⁻²)
0.79
R² vs GBOV 20 m reference maps (30 m pixels)
0.77
R² vs MODIS, scale-matched (consistency check)
Agreement with MODIS is similar across the 2000–2005, 2006–2018, and 2019–2022 periods. Field errors are largest in evergreen needleleaf forests, and LAI levels off near 5–6 m² m⁻² in dense canopies. The gap-filled product fills gaps in pixel-years with at least one retrieval but damps interannual extremes, so anomaly studies should use retrieved values (QA = 0) together with the nvalid layer.

// Earth Engine — one monthly image (int16, LAI = value * 0.001; -32768 = no value)
var lai = ee.ImageCollection('projects/ee-hyou34/assets/CONUS_Monthly_LAI_30m')
  .filter(ee.Filter.eq('year', 2010))
  .filter(ee.Filter.eq('month', 7))
  .first();
var grid = lai.projection().getInfo();   // native 30 m grid, no resampling
Export.image.toDrive({
  image: lai.unmask(-32768), description: 'LAI_2010_Jul',
  region: geometry, crs: grid.wkt, crsTransform: grid.transform, maxPixels: 1e13,
  formatOptions: {cloudOptimized: true, noData: -32768}
});
// retrieved LAI only: lai.updateMask(qa.eq(0)).unmask(-32768), qa = the matching ..._QA image;
// QA export: qa.unmask(255) with formatOptions {noData: 255}
